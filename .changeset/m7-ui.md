A new side panel: describe a change in one big field at the top (or start from an idea), watch
the steps, and see the result right under your words with a Before/After switch to flip the
page. Your site's mods sit underneath with on/off switches and a strip showing the last 10
visits; open a mod's name for its code and actions. Fresh look in light and dark, with fonts
built in (nothing is downloaded), and the settings page to match.
