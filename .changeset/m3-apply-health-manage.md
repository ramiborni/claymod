Kept mods now apply on every visit, including single-page apps. Claymod checks that each mod
still fits the page, flags mods that need repair (with a badge on the toolbar icon), and can
repair them with your model, keeping the previous version so you can undo. A new Mods tab lets
you enable, disable, rename, delete, view code, pause every mod on a site, and export or import
mods. Imported JavaScript stays off until you have read it and enabled it.
