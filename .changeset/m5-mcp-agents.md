Let the AI agent you already use drive Claymod over MCP. Turn on “Let agents use Claymod (MCP)”
in settings and add `claymod-bridge mcp` to Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode: the agent can read
the page you have open, list your mods and check their health, and propose mods. Every preview
and save waits for your approval in the side panel.
